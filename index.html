How to Play

🛡️

Block

Move your legionary up and down with the mouse, touch, W/S or the arrow keys. Catch the arrow on your shield.

🏹

Angle

Hit the arrow near the top or bottom of your shield to send it flying at a sharper angle.

⚡

Survive

Every block makes the arrow faster. Let one past and the other legion scores.

🏆

Triumph

The first legion to VII points wins. Press Space at any time to pause.
