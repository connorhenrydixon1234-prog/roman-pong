# Roman Ping Pong

Pick your warrior, choose your missile and raise your shield! Roman Ping Pong is a Roman-themed ping pong (Pong) game you can play in the browser. Block the incoming projectile with your shield, and the first to VII wins.

Play it at https://connorhenrydixon1234-prog.github.io/roman-pong/

- **Warriors:** Roman legionary, Germanic warrior or Briton warrior (pick your own and your opponent's, or a random opponent).
- **Projectiles:** arrow, pilum (javelin), spinning throwing axe or sling stone.
- **Difficulty slider:** Easy, Fair, Medium, Tough or Hard. It changes how fast the computer moves, how quickly it reacts, how well it predicts and aims, and how fast the projectiles fly.
- **Fun facts** about the Roman Empire and its emperors on the website, plus a random fact at the end of every match.

Controls: mouse, touch, W/S or the arrow keys to move. Space pauses, M goes back to the menu, Enter starts a battle from the menu.

Shortcut links: `game.html?p1=briton&p2=germanic&proj=pilum&autostart=1` (characters: `legionary`, `germanic`, `briton`, `random`; projectiles: `arrow`, `pilum`, `axe`, `stone`). Add `diff=1`–`5` or `diff=easy`…`hard` to set the difficulty. Add `demo=1` to let the computer play both sides.
